# How GOMAX compares

A quick, honest look at where GOMAX Ultimate sits next to the usual options.

## GOMAX vs. subscription AI website tools

| | Subscription AI tools | **GOMAX Ultimate** |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Monthly, forever | **Pay once** (+ optional small yearly renewal for updates) |
| Your data | Flows through their servers | **Stays on your own site** |
| AI | Locked to theirs, metered per token | **Bring your own key** (OpenAI / Anthropic / others) |
| Scope | Usually just content or a chatbot | Whole site: build, media, SEO, GEO, commerce, content, analytics, health |
| Control | Their platform, their rules | **Self-hosted — you own it** |

## GOMAX vs. page builders

- Page builders give you drag-and-drop; **GOMAX gives you a prompt** and produces premium, on-brand pages — then keeps the SEO, GEO, media and maintenance right too.
- No per-seat or per-site subscription treadmill.

## GOMAX vs. a bolt-on chatbot

- A chatbot answers visitors. **GOMAX operates the site** — it builds pages, fixes SEO/GEO, publishes content, manages the shop, and runs scheduled agents, with rollback.

## What makes GOMAX distinctive

- **Pay-once + BYOK + self-hosted** together — most tools give you at most one of these.
- **SEO *and* GEO** — findable on Google and recommendable inside AI answers.
- **Reversible autonomy** — agents that improve your site on a schedule but only make changes you can undo.
- **One system, not a stack** — building, media, SEO, commerce, analytics, health and safety in a single product.

> Comparisons describe GOMAX's own approach and design goals. Always evaluate against your own needs.
