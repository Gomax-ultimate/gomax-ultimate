# Features

Everything GOMAX Ultimate can do for your website.

## Talk to your site
Connect ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and operate your whole WordPress/WooCommerce site by chatting. No dashboards to master, no menus to hunt through.

## Build premium pages from one prompt
Describe a page and GOMAX assembles a real, on-brand experience — hero, sections, motion, imagery — that looks **designed, not generated**. Each page is unique to your brand, not a recycled template.

## Generate & optimize media
Create on-brand images from a text brief. Everything you add is automatically served as fast, modern **WebP/AVIF**, so pages stay quick on mobile.

## Get found — on Google *and* in AI answers
GOMAX keeps the technical foundations right: clean links, XML sitemaps, structured data, and the discovery files AI assistants read — so search engines rank you and assistants like ChatGPT and Gemini can **recommend** you. (See [SEO & GEO](seo-and-geo.md).)

## Sell — including to AI shopping agents
Keep your WooCommerce store discoverable and shoppable, with a machine-readable product catalog that AI shopping agents can read.

## Publish content on autopilot
Schedule SEO-ready articles that go out on a cadence — each with proper titles, meta, structured data, and instant search-engine notification. You keep the approval model you want.

## Turn on built-in agents
Switch on a named agent and it works on a schedule:
- **SEO/GEO agent** — keeps you findable on Google and in AI answers.
- **Shop agent** — keeps your store discoverable and shoppable.
- **Growth agent** — keeps your reach and freshness up.

Agents make **only reversible changes** within a risk budget, and everything can be rolled back. They never rewrite your page text on their own.

## Own your analytics & leads
First-party, privacy-first analytics and lead capture — no third-party trackers. Your visitors' data stays on your site.

## Safe by design
Changes are previewed and reversible. GOMAX is built so an AI can help run your site **without** you losing control.

## Privacy & ownership
Self-hosted. Bring your own AI key. **Pay once.** Your data never routes through someone else's SaaS.
