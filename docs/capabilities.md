# The complete GOMAX Ultimate capability list

Everything GOMAX Ultimate can do — the full picture, in plain language. GOMAX isn't a chatbot bolted onto WordPress; it's a complete **AI operating system** for your site. You talk to it, and it builds, designs, optimizes, sells, publishes, protects, and grows — across your whole WordPress & WooCommerce install.

Pay once. Self-hosted. Bring your own AI key. Your data stays on your site.

---

## 1. Talk to your site — AI control layer

- **Run WordPress in natural language.** Connect ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and operate your entire site by chatting — no dashboards to master.
- **Secure, revocable connection.** Connect an assistant with a scoped, user-bound, revocable access pass — no need to hand out an admin password.
- **Agent-native & standards-based.** Works as a modern AI/agent server and speaks the emerging WordPress agent standards, so today's and tomorrow's AI clients can connect.
- **Dual control.** Anything the AI can do, you can also do by hand from the admin — you're never dependent on the AI being available.
- **The AI understands your whole site.** It knows your brand, your pages, your products and your settings, so it acts with context instead of guessing.

## 2. Build pages & design

- **Premium page building from one prompt.** Describe a page; GOMAX assembles an agency-grade result — hero, sections, motion, imagery — that looks *designed, not generated*.
- **Whole-page composition.** Turn a single brief into a complete, multi-section page using curated, high-end page recipes filled with your own copy.
- **On-brand block generation.** Ask for any component and get a self-contained, on-brand block that drops straight into your page.
- **Premium block library.** A growing set of modern, hand-crafted blocks and components to choose from — so every site looks distinct, never templated.
- **Cinematic motion engine (Scroll Cinema).** Scroll-driven reveals, parallax, ken-burns, magnetic and scrubbed effects — GPU-cheap and reduced-motion aware.
- **Interactive & WebGL experiences.** Rich, animated, interactive sections rendered right in the page — no external builder required.
- **Brand design tokens & skins.** Consistent colors, type, spacing and shapes across everything, styleable by the AI.
- **Header, footer & menu control.** Manage site-wide chrome and navigation directly.

## 3. Media & images

- **AI image studio.** Generate on-brand images and product shots from a text brief.
- **Automatic image optimization.** Every image is served as fast, modern **WebP/AVIF**, with resizing, EXIF stripping, bulk optimization and thumbnail regeneration — with the originals kept as a backup.
- **Media management.** Organize and reuse your media library through the assistant.

## 4. Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

- **Clean, crawlable foundations.** Sensible permalinks and an XML sitemap so every page is discoverable.
- **Per-page SEO.** Titles, meta descriptions and focus keyphrases — aware of Yoast, RankMath and AIOSEO if you use them.
- **Structured data (schema.org).** Product & Offer for shops, Article for posts, Breadcrumbs, and optional FAQ — so search engines understand your content.
- **Instant indexing.** IndexNow notifies search engines the moment you publish or change something.
- **Redirect manager & 404 monitor.** Catch broken links and send old URLs to the right place.
- **Taxonomy & term SEO.** Control indexing of categories and tags.
- **One-click SEO audits.** A full-site SEO analyzer with prioritized, plain-language fixes.

## 5. GEO — get recommended inside AI answers

- **AI-discovery files.** The standard files (llms.txt, AI manifests) that let ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity understand and recommend your site.
- **AI crawler access control.** Decide which AI crawlers may read your site — allow or block each one.
- **AI Readiness scorecard.** A 0–100 score of how ready your site is for AI discovery, with ranked fixes.
- **AI-recommendation layer.** Organization and site-level data that helps assistants cite you correctly.
- **AI-visibility analytics.** See how AI is finding and engaging with your site.

## 6. WooCommerce & AI commerce

- **Keep your shop discoverable and shoppable** — by people and by AI shopping agents.
- **Machine-readable product catalog.** A live product feed AI shopping agents can read.
- **Agentic commerce (ACP/UCP-ready).** Product discovery, price quotes, checkout intent and order status for AI-driven shopping — non-custodial and standards-aligned.
- **Merchant feeds.** Native Google/Bing product feeds with a feed-to-page consistency validator.
- **Commerce-readiness scorecard.** One score across OpenAI/Google/Bing/agentic surfaces, per-product gaps, and the fixes to close them.

## 7. Content on autopilot

- **Scheduled AI publishing.** SEO-ready articles on a cadence, each with title, meta, structured data, excerpt, category, tags, internal links, a featured image and instant search-engine notification.
- **Embed video in posts.** Drop a video (a self-hosted file, YouTube/Vimeo, or an animated GOMAX hero) straight into an article.
- **Content planning & freshness.** Plan topics and keep your library fresh.

## 8. Built-in agents & autonomy

- **Agent roster.** Turn on a named agent that works on a schedule:
  - **SEO/GEO agent** — keeps you findable on Google and in AI answers.
  - **Shop Manager agent** — keeps your store discoverable and shoppable.
  - **Growth agent** — keeps your reach and content freshness up.
- **Reversible by design.** Agents make only reversible changes within a risk budget, with atomic rollback — and never rewrite your page text on their own.
- **Autopilot goals.** One-command outcomes — *fix my site*, *make me AI-discoverable*, *make me AI-commerce-ready*, *optimize SEO & GEO*, *optimize my shop*, *grow my reach* — each planned, verified, and self-reverting if anything regresses.
- **GOMAX Doctor.** Detects and safely fixes common site-breakers (broken theme setup, routing, caches, missing files) with preview and rollback.
- **Site Brain.** A persistent, self-writing knowledge base so a fresh AI chat never starts blind — it remembers your brand and your site.
- **Autonomous operator.** A daily agent that assesses, plans, acts within guardrails, and reports.
- **Workflow engine.** Trigger-based multi-step routines with approval gates.

## 9. Analytics, leads & CRM

- **First-party analytics.** Privacy-first traffic, events, engagement and intent — no third-party trackers, your data on your site.
- **Lead capture & scoring.** Capture and score leads, including automatically from chat conversations.
- **Unified inbox / CRM.** Form submissions, chat conversations, emails, leads and comments in one searchable place.
- **Outreach attribution.** See exactly which lead visited which page, per campaign.

## 10. Communication & engagement

- **On-site AI chatbot / product advisor.** A chatbot trained on your products that answers visitors and captures leads.
- **Voice agent.** Voice-driven interaction with cost controls.
- **Two-way email inbox.** Read and reply to email from inside WordPress.
- **Social engagement.** Automatic follow-backs and like-backs plus an AI reply-draft queue you approve — it never auto-posts without you.

## 11. Site health & maintenance suite

- **SEO auditor** — full-site SEO hygiene.
- **Accessibility (WCAG) auditor** — scan plus an accessibility statement generator (timely for the EU Accessibility Act).
- **Performance / Core Web Vitals auditor** — LCP, CLS, INP, TTFB and server health, no external service.
- **Link health checker** — find and bulk-fix broken links.
- **Database cleanup & optimization** — remove bloat safely, optimize tables.
- **Security hardening scorecard** — header and WordPress hardening checks with one-click, reversible fixes.
- **Backup** — native database and file backups plus off-site BYOK (S3-compatible, FTP, WebDAV) with checksums and a safe restore test.
- **Cache & HTTPS** — cache management and HTTPS/HSTS hygiene.
- **System check & hosting report** — diagnose the environment and generate a copy-paste report for your host.

## 12. Safety, control & privacy

- **Preview, verify, roll back.** Changes are previewed and reversible — the AI helps run your site without you losing control.
- **Operating modes.** Read-only, preview, or write — you set how much the AI may do.
- **Safe execution.** Snapshots and rollback around risky operations, with an audit log of what happened.
- **Privacy & ownership.** Self-hosted, first-party, **bring-your-own-key**, **pay once**. Consent management, data-subject export/erase, and data-retention controls built in.
