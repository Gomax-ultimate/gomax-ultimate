# FAQ

**Is GOMAX Ultimate a subscription?**
No. It's **pay-once**. You bring your own AI key, so there's no metered SaaS in the middle. A one-time price includes 12 months of updates; after that an optional small yearly renewal keeps updates coming — and you keep using the plugin either way.

**Where does my data go?**
It stays on your own WordPress site. GOMAX is self-hosted and privacy-first — your data doesn't route through a third-party service.

**Which AI assistants work with it?**
ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, among others. You connect the one you already use.

**Do I need to be technical?**
No. You operate your site by asking in your own words. GOMAX handles the WordPress details.

**Does it work with WooCommerce?**
Yes. It helps keep your shop discoverable and shoppable — including by AI shopping agents.

**Is it safe to let an AI change my site?**
Changes are previewed and reversible, with rollback, and the built-in agents make only reversible changes. You stay in control.

**What is GEO, and why do you mention it?**
GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is being findable and recommendable inside AI assistant answers — the AI-era counterpart to SEO. GOMAX sets up the foundations for both. See [SEO & GEO](seo-and-geo.md).

**How do I get it?**
