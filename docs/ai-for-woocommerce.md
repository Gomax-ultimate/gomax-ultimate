# AI for WooCommerce — sell to people *and* to AI shopping agents

Shoppers increasingly ask AI assistants — "find me a good X" — and those assistants recommend specific products and stores. GOMAX Ultimate keeps your **WooCommerce** store ready for that world, as well as for classic Google shopping.

## What GOMAX does for your shop

- **Keeps products discoverable and shoppable** — by people, by Google/Bing, and by AI shopping agents.
- **Publishes a machine-readable product catalog** that AI shopping agents can read.
- **Agentic commerce, ready** — product discovery, price quotes, checkout intent and order status for AI-driven shopping, non-custodial and standards-aligned (ACP/UCP).
- **Native merchant feeds** for Google and Bing, with a feed-to-page consistency validator so your listings match your pages.
- **Product structured data (schema.org)** — Product and Offer markup so search engines and assistants understand price, availability and details.
- **A commerce-readiness scorecard** — one score across OpenAI, Google, Bing and agentic surfaces, with per-product gaps and the fixes to close them.
- **An on-site AI product advisor** — a chatbot trained on your catalog that answers shoppers and captures leads.

## Put it on autopilot

Turn on the **Shop Manager agent** and GOMAX keeps your store optimized on a schedule — clean product URLs, an indexable store, products in your sitemap, and a live catalog for AI agents — making **only reversible changes** with rollback, and never touching your product text or prices on its own.

## Why it matters now

- Being invisible to AI assistants is the new "page 2 of Google."
- Early, correct AI-commerce readiness is a lasting advantage.
- GOMAX handles the technical groundwork automatically, so you can focus on selling.
