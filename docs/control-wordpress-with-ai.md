# Control WordPress with ChatGPT, Claude & Gemini

GOMAX Ultimate lets you **operate your WordPress site by talking to the AI assistant you already use** — ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. No new dashboard, no plugin maze. You describe what you want; GOMAX does it on your site.

## How it works

1. **Install GOMAX Ultimate** on your WordPress site (pay once).
2. **Bring your own AI key** — connect your own ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. No metered SaaS in the middle.
3. **Connect securely** with a scoped, revocable access pass — no admin password sharing.
4. **Just ask, in your own words.** GOMAX carries it out, with preview and rollback so you stay in control.

## Things you can ask

- "Build a landing page for my new service, with a hero and testimonials section."
- "Write and publish an SEO article about our summer opening hours."
- "Make my site discoverable in ChatGPT and Gemini answers."
- "Optimize all my images and turn on the SEO agent."
- "Add Product schema to my shop and check my Core Web Vitals."
- "Show me everyone who contacted me this week."

## Why do it through your assistant?

- **No learning curve** — you already know how to chat with an AI.
- **One place** — pages, media, SEO, GEO, shop, content, analytics, health — all from the same conversation.
- **You stay in control** — changes are previewed and reversible; you choose read-only, preview, or write mode.
- **Your data stays yours** — self-hosted, first-party, bring-your-own-key.

## Which assistants are supported?

ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, among others. Connect the one you use — GOMAX is built to be agent-native and standards-based.
