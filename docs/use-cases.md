# Use cases

GOMAX Ultimate fits anyone who wants a modern, AI-run WordPress or WooCommerce site without monthly fees or handing their data to a SaaS. Here's how different people use it.

## For agencies & freelancers
- Spin up **premium, on-brand pages from a prompt** and ship client sites faster.
- Run SEO, GEO, accessibility and performance audits in one place — deliver more value per project.
- Hand clients a site an AI can operate, while you keep control with preview and rollback.
- **Pay once per site** instead of stacking monthly subscriptions across a client roster.

## For online shops (WooCommerce)
- Keep products discoverable and shoppable — by Google, Bing **and AI shopping agents**.
- Publish a machine-readable product catalog and merchant feeds automatically.
- Turn on the **Shop Manager agent** to keep the store optimized on a schedule.
- Capture and score leads, and answer shoppers with an on-site AI product advisor.

## For creators & personal brands
- Generate on-brand images and cinematic pages without a designer.
- Auto-publish SEO articles to stay visible, with instant search-engine indexing.
- Get recommended inside ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini answers (GEO), not just Google.

## For local & small businesses
- Describe what you want in your own words — GOMAX builds and maintains it.
- Stay findable when customers ask an AI assistant for a recommendation near them.
- Keep the whole thing healthy with built-in maintenance auditors and backups.

## For privacy-conscious owners
- **Self-hosted and first-party** — your data never routes through someone else's servers.
- **Bring your own AI key** — no metered SaaS in the middle.
- Consent management and data export/erase built in.
