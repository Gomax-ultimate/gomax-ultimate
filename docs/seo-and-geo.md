# SEO & GEO — get found on Google *and* inside AI answers

Search is changing. People still Google — but they also ask **ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity**, and those assistants recommend specific businesses. Being invisible to them is the new "page 2 of Google."

**GEO** (Generative Engine Optimization) is making sure AI assistants can find, understand, and recommend you. GOMAX Ultimate does the technical groundwork for both SEO and GEO.

## What GOMAX sets up for you

- **Clean, crawlable structure** — sensible permalinks and an XML sitemap so search engines and AI crawlers can read every page.
- **Structured data (schema.org)** — machine-readable facts about your business, products, and articles, so both Google and AI assistants understand what you offer.
- **AI-discovery files** — the standard files AI assistants look for to learn what your site is and does.
- **A machine-readable product catalog** — so AI shopping agents can surface your WooCommerce products.
- **Fast, modern pages** — optimized images (WebP/AVIF) and clean markup help rankings and user experience.

## Why it matters

- Assistants increasingly **cite and recommend** businesses directly in their answers.
- Getting into those answers early is a lasting advantage — like ranking on Google before your competitors did.
- GOMAX keeps these foundations correct automatically, and its **SEO/GEO agent** can watch them on a schedule and fix reversible issues on its own.
