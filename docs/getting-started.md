# Getting started

GOMAX Ultimate lets you run your WordPress & WooCommerce site by **talking to an AI assistant** you already use. Here's the shape of it.

## 1. Install GOMAX Ultimate

Add GOMAX Ultimate to your WordPress site like any premium plugin. It's **pay-once** — no subscription.

## 2. Bring your own AI key

GOMAX doesn't lock you to one AI or meter you by the token. You connect **your own key** (OpenAI, Anthropic, or others). Your usage is your own, and your data stays on your site.

## 3. Connect your assistant

Connect **ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini**. From then on you simply *ask* — in plain language — and GOMAX carries it out on your site.

## 4. Just ask

Some things people say on day one:

- *"Build me a modern landing page for my bakery, with a hero and a menu section."*
- *"Generate a product photo of a matte-black water bottle on a stone background."*
- *"Make my site easier to find on Google and in ChatGPT answers."*
- *"Publish a short SEO article about our new opening hours."*
- *"Show me who filled in my contact form this week."*

## 5. Stay in control

GOMAX previews changes and can **roll them back**. Nothing risky happens without your say-so — you're always the one in charge.
